  controls, which then overflowed the viewport; and a fixed control `width` was also the controls'

  German setting names). Further fixed-width controls on Appearance (piece previews, a combobox, the
  swatch wrapper, both sliders, the board-theme name) were capped/wrapped/ellipsized under O3.

  real defect (pre-existing at every width) was the list's zero flex basis — fixed with
  `flex: 1 1 auto` and a content-sized grid at base. The empty Accounts state grows instead of
  centring overflow; a shared `wrap-button` class lets Button labels wrap.

  non-visible containers judged on their own (`558a12cd`); a scroller squeezed to nothing is lost
