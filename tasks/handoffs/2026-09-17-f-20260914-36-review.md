## What the plan settled

The open question — rebind at load, migrate with a schema bump, or tell the user to re-select — is
answered **rebind at load**, through the existing `acquire_target` primitive, persisted once, with a
refusal leaving the entry `Unavailable` and logged rather than failing startup. Two mechanisms were
added during review and are not optional extras: `refresh_entry` itself requires a canonical stored
spelling (otherwise the refusal is not sticky, R1-02), and an exemption is needed for entries whose
spelling is deliberately raw (R2-04, R3-02).

**The exemption took three attempts and one focused architecture judgment**, and the two withdrawn
mechanisms are recorded because each looks reasonable until measured:

1. r2 — a stored `#[serde(default)] descriptor_bound` flag set in `registration_target`'s
   `Some(expected)` arm. Withdrawn at r4: `StoredEntry` is built at three sites and
   `register_database_child` (`mod.rs:5513`) builds one without `registration_target`, and no stored
   flag can classify entries written before the flag existed.
2. r4 — containment in a `PathClass::AppOwnedRoot` entry. Withdrawn at r5: **vacuous in production**
   — `main.rs:2116-2126` passes `vec![]` and `AppOwnedRoot::new` is `#[cfg(test)]`.
3. r5, narrowed at r6 and final — containment in the six `AppOwnedDefaultRoot` directories under the
   `AppDataDir`, plus the `PathClass::AppOwnedRoot` class, rejecting any path with a `ParentDir`
   component. `starts_with(AppDataDir)` alone was refused by six lenses at r5 because the pickers
   accept arbitrary paths inside that tree.

**Settled dispositions a successor should not relitigate** (each was reported two or three times
without new evidence):

* The **residual**: a user can pick a folder or file inside one of the six app-owned directories,
  and such an entry is exempted with the application's own. It is not distinguishable by pathname,
  and separating them means giving up the lexical reuse `get_or_create_root` (`mod.rs:4790-4806`)
  and `cleanup_engine_images` (`:6060`) depend on. Owner: `f-20260905-10`.
* **Scope**: the rebinding applies to every non-exempt entry, not to a purpose allow-list. The
  MANDATE's four shapes are parenthetical examples; the defect is a property of the stored spelling.
* The **non-structural `canonical_binding` failure branch** is unreachable: `validate_target` stats
  the same pathname first (`mod.rs:3266-3284`), so anything that fails `fs::canonicalize` of the
  parent fails the earlier `symlink_metadata`. Kept as a fail-closed default with no test.
* `--lib` **runs zero tests** in this crate (`no library targets found in package 'chessfable'`);
  `--bins` is the working form. The `main.rs` startup source test is in module
  `blocking_offload_scans`, not `tests`.
