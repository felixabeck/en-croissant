# Plan: Reachable Train pane at an ordinary desktop height

At the filed 800×647 window, with the default mosaic, a person can reach Start Practice in the normal Train pane by the wheel, by keyboard focus, and by a pointer click on the button's visible box. The pane stays bounded. Build, the board tabs, practice session behavior, and the clipping assertions stay as they are.

## MANDATE

f-20261030-04. The normal practice pane clips Start Practice below its content viewport at an ordinary 800px window.

Where: src/components/panels/practice/PracticePanel.tsx line 715 sets the non-compact Train panel overflow to hidden. Its Start Practice control is at lines 967–981. The default mosaic pane has bounded height.

Failure scenario: Open an existing repertoire in an 800×647 app viewport with the normal split-pane layout. Start Practice exists and is enabled, but its rectangle top 370.1875 / bottom 412.1875 is entirely below its ancestor clipping bottom 356.1875. A physical pointer click at its box center 565,391 lands on an empty DIV. Pointerdown, pointerup and click all have originalContainsTarget false. The user cannot click this control in that pane's current visible region. Whether another ordinary scroll or focus action can reveal it is not yet measured.

Open question: Give the normal Train pane a reachable bounded content region at ordinary desktop sizes while preserving its existing layout and responsive navigation. First measure wheel, keyboard/focus and pane resizing on the existing control, then verify the selected correction through actual pointer access without weakening clipping assertions or changing snapshots blindly.

This plan does not include f-20261030-03, which owns the separate verifier error of clicking an unclipped raw center. Handled f-20261021-04 and handled f-20260829-02 are not this failure and have no shared cause established with it.

Frozen non-goals: no change to practice session rules, deck sync, tree paths, FEN identity, or copy. No change to the Build tab's scroll owner. No change to the shared board-tab clip. No new layout manager, breakpoint, or persisted layout key. No host re-record of screenshots. No restoration of removed machinery.

## Approach

### Train scrollport

The Train tab panel is the scrollport for its whole stack, in both the compact and the non-compact layout. Its used client box is the space left in the practice slot after the Train/Build tab list. It does not grow with the stack.

overflow-y is auto. overflow-x is hidden, so a tall stack scrolls vertically and does not open a horizontal document or pane scrollbar. The panel may shrink below its content (min-height 0) and take the remaining main size of the nested tab flex (flex 1, min-width 0). The nested tab root fills the practice slot (height 100%, min-height 0) and does not grow past it (overflow hidden on that root only).

The Build panel stays overflow hidden. RepertoireInfo keeps its ScrollArea. BoardAnalysis's shared panel style stays overflowY hidden and flex 1, so Analysis and the other board tabs keep today's clip.
